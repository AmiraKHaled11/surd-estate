SURD ESTATE

Grade 8 · Surds revision (Exercise 1.8A-D) · a property game

  1. Roll the dice and move around the board. A coloured property is one surd skill.
  2. Free property: answer correctly to buy it. Your property: answer correctly to add a house (more rent).
  3. Another team's property: a wrong answer pays them rent; a correct answer is safe.
  4. Pass GO: +200. Chance and Surd Cards squares draw a card. Free Parking is a bonus question. Go to Jail misses a turn.
  5. After the last round the team with the most cash + property value wins.
